## 1. D1 is extremely robust structurally

Across all five discovery eras and the untouched confirmation block:

- Extra Trees D1: positive in 5/5 discovery eras; discovery-week positive rate 1.0; untouched confirmation gain ≈ `+0.201114`.
- KNN D1: positive in 5/5; week positive rate 1.0; confirmation ≈ `+0.134396`.

Agent 6 found no single-era dependence. D1 also remained positive across all three model families in the sparse omitted-state sensitivity.

Interpretation: first-order exhaustion contains highly reproducible information about the next exhaustion behavior. This is the strongest population-level structural result.

## 2. D2 exists, but not as one universal model-agnostic structural law

54W aggregate D2:

- Extra Trees: 5/5 discovery eras positive; untouched confirmation ≈ `+0.002660`.
- Ridge: 5/5; confirmation ≈ `+0.002149`.
- KNN: 0/5; confirmation ≈ `-0.007784` in the full state.

In sparse sensitivity, KNN D2 becomes positive, showing that representation/current-state choice matters after D1.

Interpretation: do not call D2 absent merely because KNN rejects the full representation. Do not call it universally confirmed either. It is a conditional/mechanistic finding to characterize in Phase 2.

## 3. Deeper structural memory becomes increasingly model-dependent

Ridge remains weakly positive through several deeper levels; Extra Trees becomes mixed; KNN turns negative in the rich full-state view. Aggregate structural depth is therefore not a single monotonic D1→D12 law.

Phase 2 must preserve rare deeper instances rather than averaging them away.
