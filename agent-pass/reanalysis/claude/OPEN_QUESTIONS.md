## Tonight-blocking (paper execution on Tradovate)

1. **No Tradovate connection exists in this repository.** Affects every strategy's status. A repository-wide search finds no Tradovate client, credentials handling, contract mapping or order/fill logger; the only NG runner on this branch (`research/kalshi/adaptive_wavepacket_20261108/markets_bridge/ng_runner.py`) is a replay probe over the tape with no paper ledger (its own header says so). Remedy: a Tradovate demo-environment client (access token, market-data socket for the front NG contract, order socket), a deterministic paper rule (decision at the quote known at decision time, fill assumption logged, not assumed), and the decision/suppressed-signal/order/fill/exit log required by the brief. Smallest test: connect to the demo environment, subscribe to the front-month NG quote, write one minute of quotes with receive timestamps to the log, place and cancel one paper order. Benefit: moves S2 from "ready for a paper execution check" to an actual paper run.
2. **Tradovate market data is top-of-book and depth-of-market snapshots, not message-level MBO.** Affects S3 (thin-touch), S4 (touch imbalance/refill) and the dipole layers, all of which were measured on completed-group MBO reconstructions. Remedy: none tonight; those candidates stay observation-only and need a message-level feed (the Databento route used for the historical tape) if they are ever to be paper-tested. S2 (large-move fade) needs only best bid/ask and can run.
3. **Sign convention of `signed_midpoint_change_twice_raw` and the 1 ms hypothetical entry in `b-delayed-outcomes`.** Affects CLAIM M3-3 and observation-only candidate S3b. The up bias after size-one onsets on both sides is either a real asymmetry or an orientation artifact. Smallest test: recompute the 1 s midpoint change for 200 size-one signals per side from `a-trades` and the book context and compare signs with the stored field. Benefit: resolves CONFLICTS C9 before any directional use.
4. **Adverse-excursion path after a large move beyond the first 5 s.** Affects S2's stop and sizing. The packet stores the first-5 s extreme and the return clocks, not the full path. Smallest test: from the archived groups (combined-events run 42569937357 clocks, c-selected-book-context) read the 1 s midpoint path for the 225 moves to +600 s and record MAE/MFE. Benefit: an evidence-based stop instead of the provisional one on the rule card.
5. **Contract and session mapping.** The four dates are NG front-month (Nov 2023 at the time) on the CME globex session; the evidence excludes nothing around the 14:30 ET settle explicitly in the combined studies, while the house rule excludes the settle window. Remedy: the paper runner must apply the settle-window exclusion (SETTLE_UTC guard) and log it as a no-trade condition.
