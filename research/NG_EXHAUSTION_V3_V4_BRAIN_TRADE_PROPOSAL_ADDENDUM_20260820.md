## D. V4 timing proposal — timing is learned, then operationalized

The key V4 proposal is that research does **not** begin by hard-coding PRIOR/H values.

The research model scores the causal trajectory and discovers its own trustworthy decision timestamp. Only afterward is that timestamp aligned to frozen `t0` for reporting.

V4 research therefore proposes:

- no target-relative timing feature;
- no predeclared PRIOR/H grid as the primary experiment;
- every causal second is eligible for scoring in the natural stage window;
- discovery learns the lock rule;
- OOT uses the frozen lock rule;
- per-instance decision time is an output.

After enough validated V4 instances exist, a later Frankie proposal may **compress the discovered timing distributions into fixed operational rules** so Frankie does not have to rediscover stable timing relationships on every live instance.

Those operational rules may be context-specific rather than one universal number. For example, different depth/family/ancestry/regime states may support different expected recognition windows.

No operational timing map is proposed as proven yet because V4 has not run.

## E. Adaptive learning proposal — learn from experience without rewriting history

A future Frankie learning architecture should follow:

`predict -> freeze call -> reveal completed instance -> learn -> next instance`

The completed instance may update future predictive relationships, but it may never rewrite the call/timing already recorded for itself.

The V4 research contract therefore compares:

### Frozen benchmark

- discovery-trained;
- model and lock rule frozen before OOT;
- no OOT learning.

### Adaptive walk-forward

- starts as discovery twin;
- same frozen lock rule in the first adaptive study;
- each new instance starts from a model snapshot containing only earlier causally completed/revealable cases;
- current prediction freezes;
- model learns only after the instance is causally complete;
- improved state applies only to later instances.

This separation lets later adjudication answer whether adaptation genuinely adds value rather than hiding weak generalization behind continual retuning.

## F. Proposed trade architecture — evidence-gated

Trade research is downstream from validated predictive timing.

A candidate strategy must attach to the exact causal signal timestamp produced by the relevant model/target.

Allowed direction sources at that timestamp may include:

- confirmed predecessor polarity;
- contemporaneous raw price direction;
- contemporaneous signed flow/dipole direction;
- contemporaneous book/microstructure state;
- later additional causal information if separately validated.

Forbidden direction/execution inputs include:

- realized future target polarity;
- future chain membership;
- future endpoint;
- future next exhaustion identity;
- future next-exhaustion time as an exit cap.

Candidate exits must be discovery-selected fixed horizons or another independently predeclared causal management rule.

Report at minimum:

- raw/gross ticks;
- net 0.5/1/2-tick cost stress;
- MFE/MAE;
- path efficiency;
- chronological/week stability;
- validation/confirmation/held behavior;
- all losing candidates and no-trade/no-lock cases.

A historically validated candidate remains a research proposal until a later explicit promotion study/decision.
