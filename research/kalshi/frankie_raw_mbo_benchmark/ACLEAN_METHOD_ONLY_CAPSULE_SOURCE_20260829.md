the review of an earlier native replay over the same four `.mbo.dbn.zst` objects. Four of

days are now scored findings days, so there is no day in the roster a finding can be about

The earlier review is preserved unedited as the record of that run. This file exists so

## Positive knowledge capsule candidates

1. **Causal rule:** exact evidence becomes available only at F_LAST on `ts_recv_ns`. Retain
   event time, first-component receive time, F_LAST availability and decision time as
   separate clocks, and never smooth across day, family, side, session or phase identities.
2. **Parallel-view rule:** daily means supply regime and scale; exact groups, orders, FIFO
   queues, transitions and checkpoints supply causal mechanism. Neither replaces the other,
   and a valid difference between them is a `COMPLEMENTARY_SCOPE_DIFFERENCE` rather than a
   contradiction to resolve or a pooled average to take.
3. **Open-world rule:** carried family names are a crosswalk for naming what you observe.
   They are not an allowlist, not a maximum, and not a census. Anything matching no carried
   name takes a deterministic identity from its own shape and is preserved with equal
   standing, as are singletons, unmatched members and still-open structures.
4. **First-call rule:** a recognition records when something first became knowable. A later,
   better-looking recognition does not replace an earlier valid one, and a completed
   duration is not readable at a cutoff before completion.
5. **Denominator rule:** every average declares its exact numerator, population,
   denominator, source day, family, side, session, phase, continuity segment, causal clock,
   cutoff, resolved or censored status, and missingness rule. Counts, rates, quantiles,
   survival curves and ratios of sums are reported as what they are and never as arithmetic
   means.
6. **Negative-evidence rule:** abstentions, weak cases, sparse strata, censored members,
   never-observed outcomes and refuted hypotheses are retained with the same standing as
   positive findings. A hypothesis that fails is a result; a stratum too thin to support a
   claim is a result about the stratum.
