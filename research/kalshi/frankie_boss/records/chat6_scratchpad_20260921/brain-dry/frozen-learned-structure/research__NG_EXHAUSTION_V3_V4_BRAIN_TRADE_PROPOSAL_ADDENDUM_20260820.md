## A. Current proposal hierarchy

### Level 1 — methodology lessons already settled for this research line

These are proposal-worthy operating lessons because they define causal correctness, not because a trade edge has already been proven:

1. **Use the complete causal market movie.** Price direction, rolling flow/dipole, signed aggressor flow and book state remain visible continuously. Unknown future target polarity never suppresses these observations.
2. **Separate the questions.** Chain existence/continuation, eventual depth and P/O/S/X family are independent targets with independent timing.
3. **Do not make target polarity the target.** SAME/FLIP is secondary annotation/context only.
4. **Keep model evidence independent.** Logistic, ExtraTrees and KNN are not forced through a 2-of-3 vote or median probability.
5. **Preserve ancestry/root information when causally available.** Quantify root value by matched ablation rather than deleting old context by construction.
6. **Preserve true and false cases.** Regime flips, failed folds and low-support/deep cases remain part of the evidence base under `FLAG_AND_DECOMPOSE_NOT_AUTO_KILL`.
7. **Do not confuse clocks.** Retrospective `t0`, upstream live event-mark time and later endpoint confirmation are different objects.
8. **Never let a future event choose an execution exit.** Trade exits must be causal/fixed once selected.

These may eventually become Frankie reasoning doctrine, but this document itself performs no merge.

## D. V4 timing proposal — timing is learned, then operationalized

The key V4 proposal is that research does **not** begin by hard-coding PRIOR/H values.

The research model scores the causal trajectory and discovers its own trustworthy decision timestamp. Only afterward is that timestamp aligned to frozen `t0` for reporting.

V4 research therefore proposes:

- no target-relative timing feature;
- no predeclared PRIOR/H grid as the primary experiment;
- every causal second is eligible for scoring in the natural stage window;
- discovery learns the lock rule;
- OOT uses the frozen lock rule;
- per-instance decision time is an output.

After enough validated V4 instances exist, a later Frankie proposal may **compress the discovered timing distributions into fixed operational rules** so Frankie does not have to rediscover stable timing relationships on every live instance.

Those operational rules may be context-specific rather than one universal number. For example, different depth/family/ancestry/regime states may support different expected recognition windows.

No operational timing map is proposed as proven yet because V4 has not run.

## E. Adaptive learning proposal — learn from experience without rewriting history

A future Frankie learning architecture should follow:

`predict -> freeze call -> reveal completed instance -> learn -> next instance`

The completed instance may update future predictive relationships, but it may never rewrite the call/timing already recorded for itself.

The V4 research contract therefore compares:

### Frozen benchmark

- discovery-trained;
- model and lock rule frozen before OOT;
- no OOT learning.

### Adaptive walk-forward

- starts as discovery twin;
- same frozen lock rule in the first adaptive study;
- each new instance starts from a model snapshot containing only earlier causally completed/revealable cases;
- current prediction freezes;
- model learns only after the instance is causally complete;
- improved state applies only to later instances.

This separation lets later adjudication answer whether adaptation genuinely adds value rather than hiding weak generalization behind continual retuning.

## F. Proposed trade architecture — evidence-gated

Trade research is downstream from validated predictive timing.

A candidate strategy must attach to the exact causal signal timestamp produced by the relevant model/target.

Allowed direction sources at that timestamp may include:

- confirmed predecessor polarity;
- contemporaneous raw price direction;
- contemporaneous signed flow/dipole direction;
- contemporaneous book/microstructure state;
- later additional causal information if separately validated.

Forbidden direction/execution inputs include:

- realized future target polarity;
- future chain membership;
- future endpoint;
- future next exhaustion identity;
- future next-exhaustion time as an exit cap.

Candidate exits must be discovery-selected fixed horizons or another independently predeclared causal management rule.

Report at minimum:

- raw/gross ticks;
- net 0.5/1/2-tick cost stress;
- MFE/MAE;
- path efficiency;
- chronological/week stability;
- validation/confirmation/held behavior;
- all losing candidates and no-trade/no-lock cases.

- KNN: P/O/S/X is independently visible at `PRIOR age 0`; continuation reaches independent validation at T0; eventual depth does not establish an earliest timing.

`PRIOR age 0` means the first instant after all required predecessor information has causally confirmed while still before target birth. It is not a fixed number of seconds before birth.

The key research lesson is not a model vote. It is that **structural-family information can exist before continuation/depth certainty and can survive in different causal channels**. V4 must therefore preserve P/O/S/X as its own head and timing movie.

These are evidence-seeded signs that **flat channel fusion can obscure information and that nonlinear relative chain geometry may be present in the same observations but not equally extractable by every model**. ## L. Trade-translation hypothesis added from D2 Logistic

The completed D2 Logistic core/trade pair exposes an architectural gap in the current V3 trade experiment:

- core: P/O/S/X structural family is independently visible at T0;
- core: continuation has no independently validated timing;
- trade: blocks with `BLOCKED_NO_INDEPENDENTLY_VALIDATED_CONTINUATION_SIGNAL`.

The current trade translator is intentionally continuation-gated, so it cannot ask whether independently validated structural-state information should condition trade direction, entry regime or hold selection when continuation itself does not validate.

That is not a failed chain. It is evidence that predictive state and execution translation are separate layers.

A later research proposal should therefore preserve the existing continuation-driven translator and test a **separate structural-state-conditioned translator** after the relevant P/O/S/X timing has independently validated. Any such translator remains causal, model-specific, hindsight-proof and subject to the same cost/stability/validation gates.
