## Capacity

The five original October 18 Native and ROOT carriers total roughly 699 GB uncompressed for 583,688 completed groups. The available four-date span contains about 2.47 million groups. Straight scaling at the October 18 bytes per group would give roughly 3 TB uncompressed for equivalent carriers, before temporary copies, indexes and outputs. This is an order-of-magnitude estimate. A raw adapter replay and a full producer run have different costs. The separately pinned external archive is 3.79 GB compressed and includes 13.56 GB of SQLite files after extraction.
