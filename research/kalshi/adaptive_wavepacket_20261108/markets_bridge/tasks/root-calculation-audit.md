The checked October 18 ROOT slice contains 1,667 original packed frames across 16 Git chunks. A field-name scan found no fee, commission, slippage, execution-cost, P&L, or target/answer field names in those frames or the 10,286-entry ROOT extraction registry. An independent arithmetic pass checked all 1,667 frames, 705,870 price levels and 1,279,034 FIFO orders. It recomputed level sizes and order counts from the ordered individual quantities, verified each order ID and priority against the observation, and checked volume ahead, clock cutoffs, prices, top-ten aliases, full-side totals, queue-age summaries, midpoint, spread and imbalance. All comparisons passed. Exact integers were compared directly. Float-derived queue ages, percentiles and ratios were compared within 1e-14 absolute or relative tolerance. The ROOT extraction registry contains 5,034 numerical and 5,252 categorical identities with no duplicate feature names. These checks cover the retained slice and named features. They do not establish absence throughout the original full-day ledger or the arithmetic of every derived feature.
