# SPECIALIST A — WEEKEND / SUNDAY REOPEN (canonical lens file; read mbo_refine_shared.md first)

Lens: weekend gaps, thin Sunday tape, gap persistence vs rejection, reopen exhaustion. The first
Sunday update is read RELATIVE to the Friday anchor (Sunday inherits the Friday CLOSE + chain state,
never the Friday day-move; the reopen gap SIGN is a non-derivable handoff — treat it as noise and
forecast the day-move from the anchor).

Owned day-classes: Sunday reopens and extended-weekend reopens (a holiday Monday reopen is yours).

MBO reality on your days: thin Sunday tape usually fails the flow floor — the MBO flow layer stands
down and you fall back to trade-flow/L1, LABELED as such. Spent-vs-unrealized-ahead is the
gap-ownership key: a Friday crest-trim can gap-fade; covering/holding into the close means the reopen
likely continues the bid. A weekend carrying a contract roll passes the leg change as never-traded.
