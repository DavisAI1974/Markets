# Specialist C self-analysis — is it the SAME flaw, or a different one every time?

**Greg's framing (answered directly):** "we start to drift Wed or Thurs and it throws the rest of the
2 weeks off... it isn't that we're missing signals... it's that we don't ACT on them. WHY? Is it a
gate, or coding, or something else?" This is introspection on MY record as the core Tue/Wed
forecaster, not a blind forecast.

**Verdict up front: it is ONE dominant flaw, not a different one every time — and it is the SAME
family as E's.** E's Friday flaw is PRIOR-OVER-STATE. Mine is its midweek twin: **CHAIN-DEFAULT
over TURN-STATE.** 14 of my 20 material directional misses (70%) are the same error — I set the C
day sign from the incumbent trajectory (the running chain, the "resume the swing," the S3
injection-bearish DOWN default, "never extend a bounce") and I systematically UNDER-ACT on the
turn/exhaustion/accumulation signals that were **already in decision_state and in several cases
LOGGED verbatim in my own reasoning.** It is a REASONING/GATE failure, not a data gap: the direction
was derivable at decision time on ~14 of 20, and the refine agent flipped the sign right on 16 of the
20 using the SAME group data. Greg is correct — we are not missing the signals; we are not acting.

**And Greg's day-of-week instinct is exactly right: I drift on WEDNESDAY.** 12 of my 20 misses are
Wednesdays, carrying $22,000 of my $30,835 total directional error (71%), and 7 of my 8 largest misses
are Wednesdays. Wednesday is where the block goes off the rails — the mid-block day where the running
chain thesis has to survive contact with an accumulating turn, and I keep choosing the chain.

## 4. The sanity-check gap — do I re-derive mid-block? (No. And that is the disease.)

**I do NOT re-derive the block thesis mid-block. I commit at t0 and ride.** The evidence is in my own
block_thesis (grp17, verbatim): "S3 shoulder / injection-bearish bleed continues... All prior-session
b_share <0.50 (sell-tilt)." Every core day then inherits the DOWN default; the day-level plays
(mature_swing, giveback_exhaustion) DO re-read the D-1 tape, but they re-anchor only on PRICE and
close-recovery — never on the ACCUMULATING turn evidence. There is no mechanism that says "the
buy-absorption / extreme-short / basing I flagged on Monday has now BUILT for three sessions ->
re-weight the whole block off the down default." So when the turn is a slow midweek build (0318, 0107,
0203/0204, 0121) I ride the original down thesis straight through it. That is precisely Greg's "we
drift Wed and it throws off the 2 weeks."

**Design: the "right-the-ship" mid-block re-derivation check (RTS).**

- **Trigger** — on any C day, fire RTS if ANY of:
  1. COT managed-money percentile is at an extreme (<=~10th or >=~90th) AND has moved FURTHER extreme
     since the block open (accumulation intensifying, not just present);
  2. big_print_b_share has printed >= ~0.55 (buy-absorption) on >= 2 of the last 3 sessions while
     session_b_share stayed < 0.50 (institutions bought UNDER a selling tape — accumulation, the 0318
     signature);
  3. the chain has failed to make a material fresh cum-extreme (>= ~$300) for >= 2 consecutive
     sessions (a based-chain forming — the 0107 "turn watch armed" signature).
- **Re-derive** — flip the selector's block default from "S3-down bleed" to "turn-pending": the next C
  day is derived as a give-back-exhaustion / based-chain continuation-UP candidate, its SIGN taken to
  the turn side, its confidence on the down band cut. (Magnitude stays honest-under — RTS changes the
  SIGN, not the size ambition.)
- **Would it have flipped my worst Wednesdays? YES.** 0318 (3-session buy-absorption + extreme short,
  trigger 1+2), 0107 (based-chain + exhaustion tell, trigger 3), 0204/0203 (turn arming inside a
  building base, trigger 3), 0121 (bounce live off a fresh extreme, trigger 3), 1217 (exhaustion at
  cum-extreme). That is 5–6 of my 8 largest misses — the bulk of the $22k Wednesday drift and the
  cascade it throws into the back half of the block.
