The synthesis has four outputs:

1. a unified earliest-causal prediction ledger;
2. a price/root-information contribution ledger;
3. model-specific historical trade-strategy proposals from exact causal signals;
4. a curated Frankie brain proposal that remains proposal-only until deliberate adjudication and a fresh promotion contract.

## Governing causal rule

> Use every represented fact once its own causal availability time has passed. Block only hindsight/future information.

### D1-D5

- `PRIOR` is strictly before target birth `t0`.
- `PRIOR_AGE` is not H.
- H begins only after frozen target `t0`: H+1, H+2, H+3, H+4, H+5 in the first pass.
- Older predecessor occurrences retain everything already known at the global checkpoint.
- A question that validates in PRIOR does not need a later H to establish an earliest call, but later evidence is not deleted.

### D0

- D0 terminality uses `ROOT_AGE` after root causal confirmation while strictly before the next canonical event.
- D0 terminality has no descendant birth on positive cases, so its survivorship clock is never called H.
- Direct D0 terminality and D1 continuation are complementary labels on the same executable binary boundary. Their matched-probability comparison is an implementation/calibration audit, not additional independent evidence.
