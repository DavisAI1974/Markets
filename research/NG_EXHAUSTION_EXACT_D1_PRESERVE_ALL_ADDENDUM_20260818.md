# NG Exhaustion Exact-D1 Preserve-All Addendum — 2026-08-18

Status: **AUTHORITATIVE FOR THE EXACT-D1 CAMPAIGN; EVERY VALID EXACT-D1 INSTANCE IS RETAINED.**

## User directive

The exact-D1 population is not a candidate-elimination exercise. Every valid exact-D1 instance remains part of the research population. Duration, path shape, support, realized return, and cost-adjusted return are annotation/ranking dimensions only.

## Preserve-all rule

No exact-D1 row may be removed because it is:

- short, middle, long, or extreme-tail duration;
- directional or `CHOP_ROTATION`;
- less profitable than another D1;
- negative in one horizon or one validation block;
- low-support or rare;
- a true/false investigator case;
- inconvenient for a proposed mechanism.

All valid D1s remain in the master ledger.

## Required per-instance annotations

Every exact-D1 row must carry, where available:

- week and immutable event IDs;
- origin and descendant sequence indices;
- exact elapsed origin-to-descendant seconds;
- train-frozen duration-family assignment as characterization only;
- origin-wall-to-descendant causal remaining seconds;
- local pair/state/polarity grammar;
- older causal ancestry when available;
- origin event family and A-poststate context;
- descendant reference return and canonical MFE/MAE;
- gross and 0.5/1/2-tick cost-stress returns for the common reference execution;
- descendant re-origin depth/state information;
- raw-path class (`DIRECTIONAL` or `CHOP_ROTATION`) when reconstructed;
- raw-path range, MFE, MAE, endpoint displacement, efficiency, and two-sidedness when available.

## Profitability interpretation

Profitability is used to order and compare the complete D1 population, not to define membership. The campaign may rank D1 structures by repeatable causal net expectancy and may identify more-profitable and less-profitable subfamilies, but it must continue to report the full distribution and preserve every valid D1.

Historical support thresholds may be used only to grade confidence in a grouped estimate. They may not delete the underlying rows or erase low-support groups from the ledger. Low-support groups are labeled `LOW_SUPPORT`, not dropped.

## Promotion boundary

Preserve-all does not mean automatic live promotion. All D1s remain research evidence. Trade-strategy proposals may prioritize more-profitable D1 subfamilies, but no play is frozen without a separate fresh prospective/OOT promotion contract.

Permanent Frankie, Frankie 1, the detector, canonical rows, Phase-1/Phase-2 freezes, runway clock, `research/kalshi/spawn.py`, and the frozen SSOS paper play remain unchanged.
