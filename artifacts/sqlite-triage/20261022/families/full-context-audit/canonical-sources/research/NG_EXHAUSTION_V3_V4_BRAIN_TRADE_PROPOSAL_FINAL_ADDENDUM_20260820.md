## A. Proposal hierarchy correction

V3 remains useful as provenance and a diagnostic benchmark, but **its exact numerical outputs are not authoritative boundaries for the phenomenon**. The V3 line included wrong or incomplete research framing in several places, including target-relative timing buckets and directional/fixed-horizon execution questions.

For V4, the primary informational payload is the extra-agent gap analysis: what was missing, poorly represented, support-limited, model-specific, or stranded between prediction and execution.

## B. Proposed primary exhaustion brain

The future Frankie exhaustion brain should represent exhaustion as a dynamic structural process rather than a direction classifier.

Candidate primary state variables/heads:

1. **exhaustion persistence/collapse state**;
2. **runway / maturity / remaining lifespan distribution**;
3. **P/O/S/X structural state distribution**;
4. **structural continuation/termination probability**;
5. **eventual chain-depth distribution**;
6. **ordered chain-transition geometry and ancestry context**;
7. **short-run / middle-run / long-run resolution context without hard predeclared cutoffs**;
8. **continuous causal recognition/lock state and confidence**.

Direction/polarity may be causal context or a downstream execution consequence. It must not become the primary definition of whether exhaustion was measured correctly.

## C. Timing proposal correction

Do not operationalize V4 as `PRIOR -> T0 -> H+N` search.

Frankie consumes the continuous causal movie and locks when the frozen discovery-learned confidence/margin/persistence rule is satisfied. The primary time is the absolute causal lock timestamp.

Only after that ledger is frozen may historical alignment report a target-relative coordinate for analysis.

Therefore the V3 T0/H+1 discrepancy is preserved as provenance showing why fixed checkpoint scanning was the wrong approach; it is not a defect to carry into or patch for V4.

## D. Geometric/representation proposal

Keep the V4 additive geometric state, with special emphasis on the extra-agent findings:

- predecessor-to-predecessor and predecessor-to-live-state relative distances;
- normalized exhaustion trajectories;
- slopes, curvature, change-of-curvature and turning geometry;
- timing-gap ratios and chain spacing;
- dipole/flow/book response expressed relative to chain evolution;
- path-shape similarity and cross-link scale normalization;
- ordered ancestry;
- causal predecessor-overlap/resolution state;
- explicit provenance and missingness masks.

The research should test whether information previously available only implicitly to a nonlinear/local model becomes explicit and transferable through the geometric representation.

## E. Channel-fusion proposal

Do not assume flat concatenation is optimal.

Keep identifiable structural, price/path, flow/dipole and book channels or equivalent ablations. Test causal fusion/gating strategies against the flat baseline and preserve which channel first carried trustworthy information.

No cross-model vote is authorized. Model disagreement remains evidence about extraction/representation.

## F. Calibration/support proposal

Use stage-, geometry-, regime-, lifespan-, ancestry-, session- and context-conditioned support/calibration where defensible.

Do not equate sparse support with no information. D4/D5 remain case-study lanes. Preserve rare deep continuations rather than dropping them.

## G. Trade architecture correction

Trade is downstream from exhaustion-state understanding.

The earlier V3 trade grid asked questions such as with/against a direction source plus a fixed hold. Those outputs are retained as provenance but must **not score the quality of the exhaustion measurement**.

A later execution translator may consume, at the actual causal Frankie lock point:

- exhaustion persistence/collapse state;
- runway/maturity/lifespan distribution;
- P/O/S/X;
- continuation/termination probability;
- depth distribution;
- chain geometry and ancestry;
- causal price/flow/book state;
- then-current direction/polarity information if useful.

Execution research may ask direction, entry, size, hold, exit, continuation, reversal, abstention, or multi-stage management questions. But those are consequences of the measured exhaustion state, not the primary exhaustion label.

Do not require continuation to be the sole gateway to all structural-state execution research by construction.

Future target polarity, endpoint, next canonical event identity, or other hindsight remains forbidden.
